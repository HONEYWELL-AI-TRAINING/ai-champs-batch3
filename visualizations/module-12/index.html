Your Map for the Two Hours

Recap, brief, build, present, plan. The capstone is where the evidence comes together. Select a stop to jump to its first card.

Why this matters: every earlier module produced something: a spec, a test strategy, a quality gate, a tool, an agent, a trace, a cost worksheet. The capstone principle is integrate, don't restart. Link what you already made, spend the time on the gaps and on the business case, and finish with a small, owned, measured plan. Gaps you find on the way are the capstone's most useful output.
